# Runic Path
### Realms:
1. Trace Sensing
2. Rune Knowing
3. Script Visualization
4. Pattern Enlightenment
5. Rune Breath
6. Soul Inscription
7. Living Script
8. Dao Seed
9. Origin Spark
10. Infinite Script  

Higher realms would unlock deeper runes, greater understanding of existing runes, more rune slots, and longer casting time windows.  

End Goal for Path: Over a hundred Runes and a flexible Runic Sequence Casting system 
inspired by Ars Nouveau, Hex Casting, and Iron's Spells and Spellbooks, while maintaining a strong cultivation-scholar identity.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop:
>Discover rune → Observe rune → Understand rune → Record rune → Combine rune → Cast formula → Refine formula

#### Runic Path Entry Conditions
- At least Major Realm 1 in any major path
- A discovered rune source, tome, tablet, inscription, or teacher
- Maybe a "First Inscription" breakthrough ritual later


#### Types of Runes
1. Source Runes: Core runes tied to natural concepts and forces, describing the order of the world
2. Intent Runes: Action based runes that describe what things do and how they do it
3. Form Runes: Runes that outline the fundamental structure and shape of things
4. Modifier Runes: Sub-runes that alter the behavior, nature, or expression of other runes

### Splitting Runes
Runes would be denoted using common tags, and split further into deeper truths. E.g. a block like deepslate may have a broad stone rune, and then another deeper rune for being deepslate. To observe the deeper rune, the player would need a higher level of Runic Path.  

Example names: Surface Runes, Deep Runes, Hidden Runes, Origin Runes etc.  

Higher realms would expose deeper runes and hidden interactions.  

Example progression:
- Stone → Deepslate → Ancient Deep Earth
- Flame → Heat → Destruction → Solar Fire

### Archetypes of Runic Path Players:
Soft archetypes that would be encouraged through progression, discoveries, breakthroughs, and specialization.  

**Inner Rune Master** → Focuses on carving runes onto the body, soul, bones, meridians, or inner sea.  
These players would use sequences mainly around the self, such as:
- Passive buffs
- Self-healing
- Resistance
- Stat boosts
- Movement buffs
- Temporary transformation effects
- Lower range, higher survivability etc.

**Outer Rune Masters** → Focuses on writing commands onto reality itself.  
These players would use sequences mainly around exterior events and actions, such as:
- Walls
- Fields
- Traps
- Projectile sequences
- Crowd control
- Terrain manipulation
- Higher range effects etc.

## Learning a Rune: 
### States of Learning
UNSEEN Rune → The Player has no idea that this Rune exists.  
GLIMPSED Rune → The Player has briefly perceived fragments of the Rune, but barely understands it.  
OBSERVED Rune → The Player can recognize and study the Rune, but cannot cast with it yet.  
KNOWN Rune → The Player fully understands the Rune and can use it.

### How To Learn
1. Tomes, Tablets, Teachers, and ancient inscriptions can directly teach runes.
2. Runic Sight Skill lets Players observe runes embedded within:
   - blocks
   - entities
   - structures
   - spell residues
   - environmental phenomena

Observation requires focus. Losing focus disrupts progress.  

The current system uses raycasting and observation progress accumulation.

### Runic Sight Progression
0 known runes:
No passive sight. Must learn externally from tomes/tablets/teachers/discoveries.

10 known runes:
Unlock Runic Sight active skill.

25 known runes:
Common mob/entity/environmental runes become visible.

40 known runes:
Runic Sight evolves into Runic Visualization passive, making known and previously seen runes slightly visible at all times.

75+ known runes:
Complex formulas, spell residues, traps, hidden inscriptions, and layered runes become visible.

### Planned Rune Visualization
GLIMPSED:
- fragmented flickers
- unstable partial symbols

OBSERVED:
- translucent stable runes

KNOWN:
- fully visible glowing runes

Future higher mastery may:
- animate runes
- create mystical aura effects
- reveal hidden structures in reality

## Runic Casting System:
- Player becomes slowed or partially rooted
- Cannot use most other active skills
- Opens a dedicated Runic Casting UI
- Player has limited time to input a rune sequence
- Failed or incomplete formulas trigger backlash
- Casting is intended to feel tense and deliberate rather than instant

### Current Casting Direction
The current system is focused around:
- rapid rune clicking
- timed casting pressure
- sequence memorization
- visual rune organization

Current planned UI direction:
- categorized rune tabs
- scrollable rune containers
- rune icons and visual effects
- quick-access favourites later

The current direction intentionally avoids drag-and-drop casting, since it slows down rapid timed input.

### Realm Increase Bonuses
Realm 1: 2 rune slots, 3 seconds  
Realm 2: 2 rune slots, 4 seconds  
Realm 3: 3 rune slots, 5 seconds  
Realm 4: 3 rune slots, 6 seconds  
Realm 5: 4 rune slots, 7 seconds  
Realm 6: 4 rune slots, 8 seconds  
Realm 7: 5 rune slots, 9 seconds  
Realm 8: 5 rune slots, 10 seconds  
Realm 9: 6 rune slots, 11 seconds  
Realm 10: 7 rune slots, 12 seconds  

Brushes and future items may temporarily increase slot count.

### Failing in Rune Casting
- "The Runes do not align" action bar message
- Small backlash damage
- Brief nausea/disorientation
- Consumes a little qi
- Failed formulas may later destabilize the surrounding environment

### Sequence Discovery
Runic Sequences are intended to be discovered through:
- experimentation
- observation
- ancient texts
- fragmented lore
- environmental clues
- teachers and sect archives

Example:
> "Flame bound within Form, released through Piercing intent..."

Players would need to determine the missing runes themselves.  

Successful formulas become recorded inside the player's Codex.

### Mastery of Runic Sequences 
Casting the same Runic Sequence over and over may increase mastery, with grades like:
- Unstable
- Stable
- Mastered
- Perfected

Possible future benefits:
- reduced instability
- improved efficiency
- shorter cast times
- altered visuals

This is an optional future system and may not be implemented.

### Runic Items
#### Runic Codex
A tome created by a Runic Path Player which stores:
- known runes
- observation progress
- discovered sequences
- favourite formulas
- future research/lore systems

The Codex is intended to feel like a true scholarly archive rather than simply a spell list.  

The casting screen intentionally provides limited information so the Codex remains important.

#### Runic Brush
A Brush item, with different types/grades, which would give more runic slots for casting and also benefit certain runes.  

A Player currently has to hold the Brush in their Main Hand when activating the Runic Casting UI for the effect to happen.  
```
Examples: Needs work.

Stone-Tip Brush:  
Stone formulas gain stability or duration, +1 slot.

Flame-Hair Brush:  
Flame formulas gain potency, +1 slot.

Soulhair Brush:  
Soul formulas cast faster and +2 slots.

Blood-Ink Brush:  
Can replace one missing rune in a blood/self formula, but costs health, +1 slot.
```
